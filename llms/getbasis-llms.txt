# Basis

> Basis (getbasis.ai) is an AI-powered accounting platform that builds autonomous agents purpose-built for accountants. Its agents execute accounting workflows end-to-end across tax, audit, advisory, and client accounting services (CAS), shifting accountants from doing the work to reviewing it. Basis is backed by Khosla Ventures.
